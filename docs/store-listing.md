### Category
- **Primary category:** **Lifestyle**
- **Secondary category:** Business

**Recommendation and rationale:** **Lifestyle** is the primary category. Justification:
1. Real-estate/rental apps (e.g. Zoopla, Rightmove, SpareRoom) are classified under **Lifestyle** in the App Store — that is where renters actually look, so discoverability is highest there.
2. **Business** is aimed at productivity/company tools; a consumer lettings app would be lost in it.
3. The majority of our users are tenants (consumers), so the consumer-facing category wins. Keep Business as the secondary so landlord-side users still find it.

### Age rating rationale (App Store Connect questionnaire)
- Frequency: **None** for every category (no cartoon/fantasy violence, no realistic violence, no profanity/crude humour, no sexual content, no gambling, no horror/fear, no alcohol/tobacco/drug references).
- Select **"Yes"** for the question "Does your app collect personal data?" — the app collects name, phone, email, housing/employment details, and enquiry messages.
- Result: **4+** (no objectionable content; data collection is disclosed, not rated against).
- Note in the review notes: "App is a housing platform intended for users aged 18+. It does not contain objectionable content; the age rating reflects the absence of such content, and the data-collection disclosure covers the forms users fill in."

### Export compliance
- The app uses HTTPS only and **standard** encryption (Firebase SDKs). Select **"Yes — uses standard encryption"** (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false) in the build's Export Compliance section.
